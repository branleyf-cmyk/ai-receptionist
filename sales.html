Frequently asked
questions

Do I need a WhatsApp Business account? +

Yes, you'll need a WhatsApp Business number. If you don't have one yet, we'll help you set it up as part of the onboarding process — it's free and takes about 10 minutes.

Will my customers know they're chatting with AI? +

The AI is completely transparent — it introduces itself as an AI assistant for your business. Most customers actually prefer it because they get instant replies 24/7 instead of waiting hours for a human response.

How long does setup take? +

Most businesses are live within 24-48 hours. We build the chatbot, train it on your business, test it with you, and go live. You don't need to do any technical work — we handle everything.

What if the AI can't answer a question? +

The AI is smart enough to know when it can't help and will escalate the conversation to you or your team. It captures the full context so you can pick up exactly where it left off — no repeating needed.

Can I customise the AI's personality and responses? +

Absolutely. We train the AI on your brand voice, your specific services, pricing, and FAQs. You can update its knowledge anytime — add new products, change pricing, update policies — and it adapts instantly.

Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime? +

No long-term contracts. All plans are month-to-month. You can cancel anytime with 30 days notice. We're confident you'll stay because of the results, not because of a contract.
