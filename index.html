FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know about getting started with the AI Receptionist.

How quickly can the AI Receptionist be set up? +

Under 5 minutes. We connect the AI to your existing phone number — no new hardware, no apps to install, no complicated configuration. Your AI starts answering calls the same day you sign up.

Do I need technical skills to use it? +

None at all. You tell the AI about your business in plain language — your hours, services, pricing, booking rules — and it handles the rest. No coding, no dashboards to learn, no forms to fill out. Just talk to the AI like you would a new receptionist.

Can customers still reach me directly? +

Absolutely. The AI answers first and handles routine calls, but you can set it to warm-transfer to your personal line whenever needed. High-value callers or urgent situations go straight through to you — the AI knows when a human touch is required.

What happens if the AI doesn't know an answer? +

The AI is trained on your specific business information. If a question falls outside what it knows, it politely tells the customer it'll have someone follow up, captures their details, and sends you an instant WhatsApp alert. You never lose a lead.

How much does it cost? +

You only pay when calls are actually answered — no monthly subscription, no setup fees, no wasted budget on voicemails or missed calls. Our 7-day free trial lets you see real results before paying anything. Plans start from R499/month with unlimited calls.

Can it handle multiple calls at the same time? +

Yes — unlimited simultaneous calls. Unlike a human receptionist who can only handle one caller at a time, the AI manages every call instantly. During your busiest hours, not a single customer gets a busy signal.

Does it work after hours and on weekends? +

24/7, 365 days a year. The AI never sleeps, never takes sick days, and never goes on holiday. Your business can capture leads at 11pm on a Sunday — something a human receptionist can't do.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes, cancel anytime with no penalties or hidden fees. We believe in earning your business every month. If the AI isn't bringing you value, you can stop immediately — but most clients see results within the first week.

Will the AI sound robotic to my customers? +

Not at all. Our AI uses advanced natural language processing that sounds warm, friendly, and completely human. Most customers can't tell the difference. It speaks naturally, understands context, and adapts its tone to match the conversation.

How does the 7-day free trial work? +

Sign up, and your AI Receptionist goes live immediately. For 7 full days, it answers your calls, books appointments, and captures leads — completely free. No credit card required upfront. If you love the results (most do), billing starts automatically. If not, cancel with one click.
